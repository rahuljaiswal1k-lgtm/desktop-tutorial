WHY QPR MATTERS

A Quarterly Progress Report is a mandatory compliance that keeps your MahaRERA project information updated. It captures:

✓
 Construction Progress
✓
 Bookings & Inventory
✓
 Statutory Approvals
✓
 Financial Progress
✓
 Project Disclosures
Timely QPR filing helps maintain regulatory compliance, transparency and uninterrupted project operations.

WHAT MAY FOLLOW

If a pending QPR continues even after a Show Cause Notice, the recent MahaRERA order indicates that regulatory action may include:

• Minimum penalty of ₹50,000
 
• Project registration placed in abeyance
 
• Freezing of the designated project bank account
 
• Restriction on advertisement, marketing, booking & sale
 
• Restriction on registration of Agreements for Sale & Sale Deeds
 
• Restoration only after compliance and payment of the applicable penalty
